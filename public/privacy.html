Privacy Policy

Effective September 3, 2026. Casework is developed and distributed by Christopher Mancliere, an independent developer based in Canada. This policy describes the app itself; Apple separately governs the App Store and iCloud services it provides.

Information you enter

Casework stores the projects, customer jobs, contact details, site information, attachments, shop profile, libraries, prices, and preferences you choose to enter. This information is used only to provide the app's design, estimating, planning, backup, and export features.

Casework does not require a Casework account and does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.

On-device storage

Casework stores your working data in the app's private container on your device. Other apps cannot browse that container. You decide when to create a backup, export, or share a file.

Optional iCloud Sync

If you turn on iCloud Sync, Casework sends the content needed to synchronize projects, jobs, attachments, and shop settings to your private Apple iCloud database. The developer does not operate a separate server for this data. Apple processes it under your iCloud agreement and Apple’s privacy terms.

Turning sync off leaves the local and iCloud copies in place. Settings > Backup & iCloud > Remove iCloud copy deletes Casework’s private cloud zone while keeping the local copy on this device.

Backups, exports, and support

Backup and export actions create files and open Apple's share sheet. Casework does not choose or monitor the destination. The privacy practices of Mail, Files providers, cloud drives, or other destinations you select are governed by those services.

If you contact support, the developer receives only the information you deliberately send. Remove customer or project information that is not needed to explain the problem.

Retention, deletion, and choices

Local projects and jobs remain until you delete them, replace them during a restore, or remove the app and its data. You can delete individual work inside Casework and can create a portable backup before doing so.

You may request access to or deletion of information you previously sent directly to support using our support form.

Security and changes

Casework uses Apple's application sandbox and CloudKit security controls. No method of electronic storage is completely risk-free, so keep a separate current backup of important work.

Material changes to this policy will be published with an updated effective date.

Contact

For support or questions about this privacy policy, contact Christopher Mancliere through our support form.

Website and contact form

This website uses Cloudflare to deliver pages, protect requests, and process contact-form submissions. When you submit the form, we receive your email address, optional name, selected topic, and message so we can respond. Messages are delivered to the developer’s support mailbox, hosted by Google. The form does not upload files or read data from the Casework app.

Cloudflare processes network information, including your IP address, for delivery and abuse prevention. The website adds no advertising trackers or analytics cookies. Your form entries are not stored in browser storage or an application database by this website; they remain in the page while you compose or retry a message.

Support correspondence is kept as needed to respond to and resolve your request and meet applicable obligations. You can request access or deletion using the contact form and choosing Privacy or data request. Please avoid sending unnecessary customer or project information.
